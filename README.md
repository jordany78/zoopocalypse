# Zoopocalypse

**Zoopocalypse** is a **top-down arena shooter roguelite** where you play as a **zookeeper** fighting off **hordes of mutant animals**.

---

## Game overview

The zoo has gone terribly wrong. Mutated animals are overrunning the grounds, and it’s your job to survive the outbreak, clear waves of enemies, and keep pushing forward as the chaos escalates.

**Core features**
- **Top-down arena combat** against increasing hordes
- **Roguelite progression** with replayable runs
- **Mutant animal enemies** with escalating difficulty

---

## How to play

1. **Move** around the arena to avoid getting overwhelmed.
2. **Fight** mutant animals and survive each wave.
3. **Progress** through your run and adapt as enemies get tougher.
